Questions

What makes a logo design "luxury"?
Luxury logo design is characterized by restraint, typographic precision, and intentional use of space. It is not about gold colors or ornate decoration — it is about the visual discipline to communicate premium value through simplicity. A luxury designer understands how composition, spacing, and typeface selection create a perception of quality before any content is read.
