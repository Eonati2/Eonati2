## Patterns
- 100% of top listings are Canva-native (Canva link in a PDF). "Canva Template" in nearly every title.
- Title formula: [style word] Wedding Invitation Bundle/Suite - [scope] - Editable Canva Template - Instant Download/Printable. Trending style word: "Old Money" (5 of the top bundle titles), also Luxury, Minimalist, Sage Green, Burgundy.
- 10-20 listing photos each (ours: 6 covers; Gumroad max 8).
- Big counts sell the scope: "Bundle of 36", "145 matching templates".
- Most show a permanent ~50% "sale" price (we do NOT do fake sales - owner rule).
- Descriptions: "what's included" list, "how it works" steps, link to matching items.

## Verdict for our $34 suite
- Price: right in the middle of real bundle prices ($22-55 paid). Keep $34.
- Gap 1: Canva-native link (we use PowerPoint import into Canva). Owner declined Canva Pro template links (2026-11-23).
- Gap 2: fewer photos (6 vs 10-20); 2 cover slots left.
- Applied: Gumroad title now starts "Wedding Invitation Bundle" and puts "Canva" before Google Slides.
