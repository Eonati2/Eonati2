Updated 2026-11-28. Owner-approved changes only; every ads/price/discount change still needs the owner's words.

## 2. Decision rules (when to wait, when to judge, when to edit)

| Metric | Wait until | Then |
|---|---|---|
| Learning period | ~Dec 3 | No budget/keyword changes before, except clear waste with owner yes |
| Ad click rate | ~1,000 impressions per ad | Under 0.5% = recommend swap (backups SU20, SU24, SU17) |
| Page engagement | 30-40 ad visits | Still ~0 engaged = page/offer problem, propose fix |
| Add-to-cart | ~60 clicks | 0 add-to-cart = recommend pause and rethink |
| Scaling | Cost per sale < ~$20 | +20-30% budget every 3-4 days (owner yes) |
| Conversion campaign | ~50 checkouts/week + tag confirmed | Switch from traffic to sales objective |
| Freebie | ~50 downloads | Count freebie -> suite clicks and sales; 0 = change offer or stop |
| Organic | ~Dec 6 | Compare weekly organic views vs 58/week low |

## 3. The math to $10k
- $10,000 / ~$30 net per sale = ~340 sales/month (~11/day).
- At $1 per click and 3% buying: ~$33 ad cost per sale -> losing money. At $0.50/click and 5%: $10 per sale -> profitable.
- So the order is: 1) first sales, 2) raise buying rate (reviews, retargeting, freebie), 3) lower click cost (better ads, 25-44), 4) only then scale budget.
- Milestones: first sale -> 10 sales (reviews start) -> 1/day -> 3/day -> 11/day.

## 4. Season (important)
Engagement season runs Thanksgiving to Valentine's; many couples get engaged in December and start planning in January, when save-the-date and invitation searches rise. Goal: have proof (first sales, reviews, a winning ad) by late December so the budget can grow in January.

## 5. Not doing (and why)
- Raising the price (0 reviews; $34 sits in the $22-46 competitor band).
- Fake urgency, fake reviews, "was" prices.
- Paid ads for the freebie (no direct revenue).
- Putting the freebie on the suite page (would distract ready buyers; revisit with data).
