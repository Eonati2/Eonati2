Questions

What makes a good restaurant logo?
A good restaurant logo communicates the dining experience — cuisine type, price point, and atmosphere — before the customer steps inside. It works at every size from a delivery app icon to building signage. It has a distinctive mark that isn't built on generic food imagery like forks or chef's hats. And it passes the black-and-white test for mono print applications.
What colors work best for restaurant logos?
Red and orange are appetite stimulants — right for fast food and casual dining. Warm earth tones work for artisan and authentic cuisines. Green signals fresh and healthy. Black and gold signal fine dining and premium positioning. Blue is the one color to avoid in most food contexts — it's an appetite suppressant. Match your color to the dining experience you are actually selling.
How much does a restaurant logo design cost?
A professional restaurant logo from a Fiverr Pro verified designer starts at €23. A complete brand identity including menu typography starts at around €112–€200. Agency restaurant branding typically costs €1,500–€10,000 for comparable scope. Fiverr Pro includes full vector files, commercial rights, and revision rounds — everything needed for print, signage, and digital use.
Should a restaurant logo include food imagery?
Not necessarily. Many successful restaurant logos use no food imagery — relying instead on typography, abstract marks, or monograms. Food imagery works when it is distinctive (not a generic fork or chef's hat) or when it directly references a signature dish. Generic food imagery makes your logo look identical to every competitor in your category.
