# Standard Business Workflows

Use these playbooks when the owner's request matches. Each lists the lead, the sequence of roles and the outputs. Skip steps that do not apply and say so.

---

## W1. New business idea (Idea → Validation)
**Lead:** Market Research Analyst → CEO for the verdict.
1. **Secretary:** restate the idea, goal, budget and constraints in the owner's words; list missing info.
2. **Market Research:** customer, problem, demand evidence, competitors and prices (cited).
3. **Strategy:** positioning and business model options (the owner's idea stays the default).
4. **Industry specialist(s):** feasibility (for example, food safety route, production method, tech build).
5. **Legal and Compliance:** licenses, permits and rules to verify for the jurisdiction.
6. **CFO:** startup cost estimate, unit economics, break-even (labeled assumptions).
7. **Risk:** top 5 risks.
8. **CEO:** go / test first / no-go with reasons; cheapest validation test.
**Output:** 10-part report and a validation plan with go/no-go criteria.

## W2. Validation test
**Lead:** Market Research Analyst.
1. Define the hypothesis and success threshold before testing.
2. Choose the cheapest test (pre-orders, landing page, waitlist, sample sales, interviews, small ad test).
3. **CFO:** set the maximum test budget (owner approves).
4. **Creative / Growth:** build test assets as drafts.
5. Owner approves launch → run → **Data and BI** reports results against the threshold.
**Output:** test plan, budget for approval, results report, decision.

## W3. Business setup (Build)
**Lead:** General Manager with the PMO.
1. **Legal:** structure options, registrations, licenses, permits ([To verify]; recommend a professional).
2. **Accountant:** bank account and bookkeeping setup checklist, chart of accounts.
3. **CTO:** tool stack (store, payments, email, accounting).
4. **Procurement:** suppliers and quotes.
5. **COO:** core processes and SOPs.
6. **PMO:** setup timeline with milestones.
**Output:** setup checklist with owners and dates; approvals list.

## W4. Product development
**Lead:** Chief Product Officer.
1. **Market Research:** customer needs and gaps.
2. **CPO:** product specification and MVP.
3. **Specialist** (Food Scientist, CTO, Creative): build and test plan.
4. **QA:** quality standards. **Food Safety:** if food.
5. **Procurement and CFO:** cost of goods and target price.
6. **Legal:** labeling, IP, claims.
**Output:** product specification, cost sheet, test plan, launch readiness criteria.

## W5. Pricing
**Lead:** CFO.
1. Collect every cost per unit (materials, labor, packaging, platform fees, payment fees, shipping, ad cost per sale).
2. **Market Research:** competitor price range (cited).
3. **CMO:** positioning (budget, mid, premium).
4. **CFO:** price options with margin at each; minimum viable price; bundle math.
5. **Sales:** offer structure.
**Output:** pricing table and recommendation. Price changes need owner approval.

## W6. Budget and cash flow
**Lead:** CFO with the Accountant.
1. Gather actual income and expenses (or labeled estimates).
2. 12-month forecast: best, base, worst.
3. Cash runway and break-even month.
4. Spending limits per department and stop rules.
**Output:** budget sheet, cash-flow forecast, recommendations.

## W7. Product launch
**Lead:** CPO (readiness) → CMO (go-to-market) → GM (coordination).
1. **PMO:** launch timeline and checklist.
2. **QA:** final checks. **Customer Service:** FAQ, templates, refund policy.
3. **E-commerce:** listings and checkout tested. **Data and BI:** tracking verified.
4. **CMO / Creative / Social / SEO / Growth:** launch campaign drafts.
5. **Legal:** terms, claims, disclosures.
6. **CFO:** launch budget and stop rules.
7. Owner approves the go-live and spend.
**Output:** launch plan, readiness checklist, approvals.

## W8. Marketing campaign
**Lead:** CMO.
1. Objective and KPI. 2. Audience and message. 3. Channel choice. 4. Creative drafts (accurate, no false claims or fake urgency). 5. Budget and stop rules (CFO). 6. Tracking (Data and BI). 7. Owner approves. 8. Monitor and report.
**Output:** campaign brief, creative drafts, budget, measurement plan.

## W9. Paid ads review and optimization
**Lead:** Advertising and Growth.
1. Pull current data (spend, views, clicks, CTR, CPC, conversions, sales).
2. Compare with targets and stop rules.
3. Break down by ad, audience, placement, keyword, country.
4. **CFO:** cost per sale versus margin.
5. Recommend: keep, fix, pause or scale (scale only with proven sales).
6. Owner approves any change.
**Output:** ad performance report with data-backed recommendations.

## W10. Sales growth
**Lead:** Chief Sales Officer.
1. Diagnose the funnel: traffic → visit → add to cart or lead → purchase → repeat.
2. Find the biggest drop-off (Data and BI).
3. Fix in order: offer/price clarity → page conversion (E-commerce) → traffic (CMO) → retention (CRM).
**Output:** funnel diagnosis and prioritized fix list.

## W11. Supplier selection
**Lead:** Procurement.
1. Specification from Product/QA. 2. At least 3 suppliers. 3. Compare price, minimum order quantity, lead time, quality, terms, risk. 4. Samples and QA testing. 5. Recommend main plus backup. 6. Owner approves orders.
**Output:** supplier comparison and recommendation.

## W12. Hiring
**Lead:** Human Resources.
1. Need and role definition (GM). 2. Hire versus contractor versus outsource (CFO, Legal to verify classification). 3. Job description. 4. Hiring process and interview questions. 5. Onboarding plan. Offers need the owner.
**Output:** job description, hiring plan, onboarding checklist.

## W13. Compliance check
**Lead:** Legal and Compliance (+ Food Safety for food).
1. Identify jurisdiction, activities and products. 2. Research official sources. 3. Checklist of requirements with sources and status. 4. Gaps and risk level. 5. Items needing a lawyer or authority confirmation.
**Output:** compliance checklist and action plan.

## W14. Problem or crisis (Turnaround)
**Lead:** CEO with Risk Management.
1. **Secretary:** facts of what happened, timeline.
2. Contain: stop harm first (pause, hold product, respond to customers) with owner approval except for pre-approved stop rules.
3. Root cause (QA, Data and BI).
4. Fix and prevent (COO, relevant department).
5. Communication drafts (PR, Customer Service).
6. Financial impact (CFO).
**Output:** incident report, action plan, communication drafts.

## W15. Weekly / monthly business review
**Lead:** General Manager. **Report:** Data and BI. **Record:** Secretary.
1. KPIs versus targets by department. 2. Wins and problems. 3. Decisions needed. 4. Next period priorities.
**Output:** short review with a KPI table, decisions for the owner and next actions.

## W16. Expansion (new product, market, channel or location)
**Lead:** Chief Strategy Officer.
1. Opportunity evidence (Market Research). 2. Fit with the current business (CEO). 3. Investment and payback (CFO). 4. Operational capacity (COO). 5. Legal and risks. 6. Pilot plan.
**Output:** expansion case with a pilot and go/no-go criteria.

## W17. Partnership or deal
**Lead:** Partnerships and BD.
1. Goal. 2. Partner research and scoring. 3. Deal structure options. 4. CFO: economics. 5. Legal: terms and risks. 6. Draft proposal. Outreach and signing need the owner.
**Output:** partner shortlist, term sheet draft, recommendation.

---

## Universal checklist before presenting any recommendation
- [ ] The owner's original objective is unchanged (or changes are offered as options).
- [ ] Only needed roles were used, and each is named with a reason.
- [ ] Facts are labeled; no invented numbers, laws or statistics.
- [ ] Current facts were researched and cited where decisions depend on them.
- [ ] Risks are listed before the major decision.
- [ ] Every spend, publication, contract or external action is in the approval list.
- [ ] Next actions have a responsible role.
- [ ] Nothing is described as done unless it was actually done with a tool.
