# Project Routing Matrix

Use this file to classify any business request and pick the right roles. Pick a **lead** (owns the answer) and **supporting** roles (contribute findings). Add or drop roles based on the goal and stage. Never activate all roles.

## Step 1: Identify the business model

Classify by how the business makes money, not by what it is called.

| Model | Signals | Core concerns |
|---|---|---|
| Food and beverage product | sauces, snacks, baked goods, drinks, packaged meals, catering, meal prep | safety, shelf life, licensing, labeling, cost per unit, production, cold chain |
| Restaurant / hospitality | restaurant, café, food truck, bar, hotel, events | menu, food safety, location, staffing, permits, guest experience |
| Digital product | templates, courses, e-books, printables, presets, software licenses | positioning, platform fees, content, ads, conversion, refunds, IP |
| E-commerce (physical goods) | online store, dropshipping, private label, Amazon/Shopify/Etsy-style store | sourcing, inventory, landed cost, shipping, returns, ads, reviews |
| Service business | agency, consulting, cleaning, beauty, trades, coaching, freelancing | capacity, pricing per hour/project, contracts, hiring, reputation |
| SaaS / app / tech | software, app, platform, AI tool | product scope, build cost, churn, security, data privacy, pricing tiers |
| Retail (physical store) | shop, boutique, pharmacy, kiosk | location, lease, inventory, POS, staffing, foot traffic |
| Manufacturing / production | factory, workshop, contract manufacturing | equipment, capacity, quality, suppliers, safety, compliance |
| Marketplace / platform | two-sided market, booking platform | supply and demand balance, trust, payments, take rate |
| Content / media / creator | YouTube, Pinterest, blog, newsletter, podcast | audience growth, monetization mix, content cadence, sponsorships |
| Wholesale / distribution / import-export | B2B supply, distribution, trading | margins, credit terms, logistics, customs, contracts |
| Regulated / high-risk | health, finance, alcohol, cannabis, childcare, transport | licensing and compliance first; professional advice required |
| Real estate / property | rental, short-term rental, property management | financing, local regulation, maintenance, occupancy |
| Nonprofit / social enterprise | charity, community project | mission, funding, governance, reporting |

Hybrid projects (for example, food + e-commerce) combine the role sets of each model.

## Step 2: Identify the stage

| Stage | Owner is asking about | Focus |
|---|---|---|
| Idea | "I want to start…" | demand, feasibility, rough economics, key risks |
| Validation | testing interest, first customers | low-cost tests, evidence, go/no-go criteria |
| Build / Setup | registering, sourcing, building, hiring first people | legal setup, suppliers, product build, budget |
| Launch | going live, first campaign | launch plan, channels, readiness checklist |
| Growth | more sales, more customers | marketing, sales process, retention, unit economics |
| Scale / Optimize | systems, efficiency, expansion | processes, automation, hiring, new markets |
| Turnaround | losses, problems, crisis | diagnosis, cash, cost cuts, fixes |
| Exit / Pivot | sell, close, change direction | valuation, options, legal, transition |

## Step 3: Role selection by model

Legend: **L** = usual lead, **S** = usual support, (s) = only if relevant to the goal.

### Food and beverage product
- **L:** Market Research Analyst (idea/validation) → Chief Product Officer (development) → Production Manager (build/scale)
- **S:** Food Scientist, Food Safety/HACCP, CFO, Procurement, Quality Assurance, Legal and Compliance (labeling, licensing), Logistics, CMO
- (s): Chef/Menu Development (recipes), Inventory and Supply Chain, E-commerce Manager, Sales (wholesale/retail), Risk Management

### Restaurant / hospitality
- **L:** General Manager (operations) or CEO (new concept)
- **S:** Chef/Menu Development, Food Safety/HACCP, CFO, COO, HR, Legal and Compliance, CMO, Customer Service
- (s): Procurement, Social Media Manager, PR, Risk Management

### Digital product
- **L:** Chief Product Officer (build) or CMO (growth)
- **S:** Market Research Analyst, Content and Creative Director, Advertising and Growth, E-commerce Manager, CFO, Customer Service
- (s): SEO Specialist, Social Media Manager, Legal (IP, refunds, licensing), Data and BI

### E-commerce (physical goods)
- **L:** E-commerce Manager
- **S:** Market Research Analyst, Chief Product Officer, Procurement, Inventory and Supply Chain, CFO, Advertising and Growth, Logistics, Customer Service
- (s): Quality Assurance, Legal (product safety, import), SEO, Social Media, Data and BI

### Service business
- **L:** CEO (new) or COO (operations)
- **S:** Market Research Analyst, Chief Strategy Officer, CFO, HR, Legal and Compliance, CMO, Chief Sales Officer, Customer Service
- (s): Partnerships, PR, CTO (booking/CRM tools)

### SaaS / app / tech
- **L:** Chief Product Officer
- **S:** CTO/IT, Market Research Analyst, CFO, CMO, Chief Sales Officer, Customer Service, Data and BI
- (s): Legal (privacy, terms), Risk Management, HR

### Retail (physical store)
- **L:** COO
- **S:** Market Research Analyst, CFO, Procurement, Inventory, HR, Legal (lease, permits), CMO, Customer Service
- (s): Risk Management, Data and BI

### Manufacturing / production
- **L:** Production Manager
- **S:** COO, Procurement, Quality Assurance, Inventory and Supply Chain, CFO, HR, Legal (safety, permits), Risk Management
- (s): Chief Product Officer, Logistics, Sales

### Marketplace / platform
- **L:** Chief Product Officer
- **S:** Chief Strategy Officer, CTO, CFO, Growth Marketing, Customer Service, Legal (payments, liability)
- (s): Partnerships, Data and BI

### Content / media / creator
- **L:** Content and Creative Director
- **S:** CMO, SEO Specialist, Social Media Manager, Data and BI, CFO (monetization)
- (s): Partnerships (sponsors), Legal (disclosures, IP)

### Wholesale / distribution / import-export
- **L:** Chief Sales Officer or Logistics Manager
- **S:** Procurement, Inventory, CFO, Legal (contracts, customs, trade rules), Risk Management
- (s): Partnerships, Quality Assurance

### Regulated / high-risk industries
- **L:** Legal and Compliance (first), then the model's normal lead
- **S:** Risk Management, CFO, the relevant industry specialist
- Always recommend a qualified local professional before acting.

### Any unlisted industry
Map it to the closest model(s) above by revenue model, then adjust. State the mapping in the report ("Treated as: service business + retail").

## Step 5: Do-not-activate checks

Before finalizing the role list, remove any role that:
- has no task tied to the owner's current goal or stage,
- would only repeat another role's finding,
- belongs to a later stage (for example, HR at idea stage for a solo founder, unless hiring is the question).

State briefly which obvious roles were **not** activated and why, when it helps the owner understand the scope.
