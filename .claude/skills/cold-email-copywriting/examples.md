# Cold Email Copy — Examples

Business-agnostic patterns. Replace bracketed placeholders with user-provided context. Do not invent metrics.

---

## Example 1: Healthcare operations (fictional)

**Before (reject):**

```
Hi {{firstName}},

I hope this email finds you well! I came across your profile and love what
[Hospital Name] is doing in patient care. We are a leading healthcare technology
provider helping hospitals transform outcomes. Our platform integrates with
EHR systems and has helped many organizations. Would you be open to a 30-minute
call next Tuesday to discuss how we can help?

Best,
[Sender]
```

**After (approve):**

```
Hi {{firstName}},

Post-discharge readmissions often spike when follow-up coordination between
wards and community care breaks down — especially across multi-site systems.

We helped a regional health network tighten that handoff and shorten readmission
windows — if I put together a one-page outline of how that mapped to their ward
structure, would it be useful to send over?

[Sender]
```

Why it works: specific pain, proof supplied by the user (never invent metrics), low-friction CTA, no links, no fluff.

## Example 2: B2B SaaS — engineering leaders

**Before (reject):**

```
Hi {{firstName}},

I noticed we both went to [University] — small world! I saw your company is
growing fast and thought our DevOps platform could be a great fit. We offer
CI/CD, monitoring, and security in one tool. Can we hop on a quick call?
```

**After (approve):**

```
Hi {{firstName}},

Teams your size usually hit deploy friction when preview environments don't
match prod — bugs slip through and releases slow down.

We helped a 40-person eng team get preview parity in a week without changing
their Git flow. If I recorded a 90-second walkthrough of that setup, would
you want me to send it?

[Sender]
```

## Example 3: Low-friction CTA variants

Same bridge, different CTAs — pick based on user comfort and asset type:

```
A) If I could show you what that looks like through a quick personalized
   example, would that be valuable?

B) Would you be open to me sharing a short video on what that actually
   means for [company]?

C) Do you mind if I send that right over?
```

All three avoid calendar pressure and invite a one-word reply.

## Example 4: Hook patterns (fill-in)

| Pattern | Template |
|---------|----------|
| Trigger event | "Saw [company] just [raised / hired / expanded into X] — that usually creates [specific ops pain]." |
| Role pain | "[Role]s at [company type] often struggle with [concrete problem] when [condition]." |
| Industry constraint | "[Regulation / market shift] is pushing [ICP] to [required change] faster than most teams planned." |
| Observable signal | "Companies running [stack / model] typically see [bottleneck] once they pass [scale signal]." |

Hooks must be **plausible and researched** — user confirms accuracy before send.

## Example 5: Follow-up (no "just checking in")

```
Hi {{firstName}},

Different angle — [new insight, stat, or mini case] relevant to [their situation].

Still happy to send [asset] if useful. Want me to?
```

New value every touch. Same low-friction CTA.
