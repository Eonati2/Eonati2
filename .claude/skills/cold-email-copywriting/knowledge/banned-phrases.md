# Banned Phrases & Patterns (Auto-Reject)

> If any cold email contains these, reject the draft and rewrite. Non-negotiable.

## Subject lines

- "Quick question" / "Following up" / "Checking in" / "Touching base"
- Fake "Re:" when it is not a reply
- "FREE", "$$$", ALL CAPS, `?!`, emojis (almost always)
- More than ~7 words or generic categories ("Marketing question")

## Opening lines

- "I hope this email finds you well"
- "Hope you're having a great day"
- "I wanted to reach out because"
- "I'm [Name] from [Company]" / "We are a leading provider of"
- "I came across your company while" / "I'm sure you're busy"
- "Apologies for the cold outreach"

## Body content

- "compare notes" — passive, never works
- "leverage", "synergy", "best-in-class", "next-gen", "transform", "empower"
- "revolutionize", "cutting-edge", "innovative", "world-class", "game-changer"
- "robust", "seamless", "scalable solution", "frictionless"
- "best practices", "thought leadership", "actionable insights", "value-add"
- "circle back" / "loop back"

## Punctuation & formatting

- Em dashes (—) anywhere
- `$` symbol (spam trigger) — prefer "no cost" / spell out USD if required
- Multiple exclamation marks, heavy bold/italics, bullet lists in email 1
- HTML tables, shortened URLs, multiple links in first touch

## CTAs

- "Would you have 15 minutes for a quick call?"
- "Could we hop on a call?" / "Open to chatting?" / "Worth a conversation?"
- "Calendar link below" / "Click here to book" with no value preview
- "Looking forward to hearing from you"

## Unsourced proof

- Specific percentages or dollar savings without a source
- "We helped 100+ companies" if you cannot name a few
- "Trusted by Fortune 500" / "award-winning" without proof

## Patterns that scream AI

- Three-bullet "Here are three reasons…" structures
- "In today's [industry] landscape"
- Perfectly parallel sentence structures
- "Moreover," "furthermore," "additionally"
- Closing with "Best regards," / "Warm regards," / "Looking forward"

## Litmus test

Would the real sender type this on their phone in 30 seconds — or is it AI cosplaying as a salesperson? If it fails that test, rewrite or delete.

## Quick replacements

| Banned | Prefer |
|--------|--------|
| leverage | use |
| transform | change / rebuild / replace |
| seamless | without downtime / no cutover gap |
| 15-minute call ask | Conditional Question (see `conditional-question.md`) |
| em dash | comma, period, or new sentence |
| $ | "no cost" / spell out |
