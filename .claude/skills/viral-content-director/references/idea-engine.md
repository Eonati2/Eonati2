# Idea engine

Editing has a ceiling set by the idea. This is where most of the leverage is, and where
almost nobody spends their effort.

## Generate 20, and make them actually different

Twenty rewordings of one premise is one idea. Force spread by walking these axes and
taking each somewhere different:

- **Premise** — what is claimed or shown
- **Emotion** — awe, unease, nostalgia, envy, relief, indignation, tenderness, dread
- **Angle** — insider, outsider, victim, expert, sceptic, convert, bystander
- **Mechanism** — reveal, comparison, countdown, transformation, process, mistake,
  confession, warning, demonstration, contradiction
- **Promise** — what the viewer gets for staying
- **Visual treatment** — how it will actually look

If two concepts share three axes, one of them is redundant. Replace it.

## Score every concept

| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Idea strength | ×3 |
| Curiosity | ×3 |
| Retention potential | ×3 |
| Visual potential | ×3 |
| Novelty | ×2 |
| Emotional potential | ×2 |
| Shareability | ×2 |
| Rewatchability | ×2 |
| Platform fit | ×1 |
| Audience fit | ×1 |
| Commentability | ×1 |
| Production feasibility | ×1 |

Each /10. Max 240. Feasibility is weighted lowest deliberately — convenience is the
commonest reason a weak idea survives, and it is the worst reason.

**Under ~150: do not proceed.** Generate another 20. A great edit cannot rescue a weak
idea, and the hours go in anyway.

Log the winner *and* the near-misses — today's second place is often next week's first,
and the losers stop you regenerating the same dead ends.

## Hook lab — 15 per concept

Score each /10 on: instant clarity · curiosity · novelty · emotional tension ·
specificity · visual potential · credibility · payoff potential.

The first frame and the first line are one unit. A strong line over a dead frame fails,
because the frame arrives first.

Working shapes: contradiction of a held belief · a number that sounds wrong · an unfinished
action · a visible stake · a confession · a warning with a deadline · a before that
implies an after · a question only this video answers · something physically strange on
screen.

Dead on arrival: "Hey guys" · "In this video" · "Let me tell you about" · "Did you know" ·
any branded intro · anything the viewer has to be polite to sit through.

**The hook must be honest.** A hook the video does not pay off buys one view and costs the
account, because the drop-off teaches the algorithm the clip is bait.
