# Free Product — Prospecting Starter Kit

Top of funnel. Two files, given away for an email address.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `COMMERCIAL-CLEANING-PROSPECTING-STARTER-KIT.pdf` | 25 buying triggers, three outreach emails, how to use the tracker |
| `ACCOUNT-TRACKER-LITE.xlsx` | One tab, twenty rows, the seven filters, a four-part score |

Source: `src/`. Nurture sequence: `../commercial-cleaning-os/outbound/sequences/free-product-nurture.md`.

## The test it has to pass

Someone who downloads it and never buys anything should still be glad they did.

It gives a complete slice: find twenty accounts, spot a dated trigger, send an email. That works on
its own. What it doesn't give is the system around it — the full scoring model, the claim table,
the eleven-tab tracker, reply handling, the walkthrough checklist, the pricing method, the proposal
structure, the scoreboard.

**Free answers "does this work?" Paid answers "how do I run this every week without it falling
apart?"** That's a real difference, not a paywall dropped mid-sentence.

## Where it sits

The free kit is **not** the first touch. The first touch is still an offer to build a
trigger-matched list for their own city — personal and specific, where a generic download isn't.
The kit goes out *with* that pack, so they get something immediate and something reusable.

It also works standalone for organic distribution, where there's no pack to build.

## Audited

No fabricated proof, no download counts, no urgency, no testimonials — there are no customers yet.
No pricing, no internal references. 25 triggers, all with a source and a freshness window.
