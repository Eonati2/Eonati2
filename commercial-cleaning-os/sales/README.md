## The three rules running through all of them

**Never quote before you have seen the building.** Discovery qualifies; the walkthrough prices.
A phone quote is padded, and padded quotes compete only on padding.

**A claim about the prospect must trace to something they said or something observed and dated.**
The same standard as outreach copy, enforced all the way into the proposal.

**Ask why on every loss and record it as a code, not prose.** One loss is noise; ten coded losses
are a diagnosis. Free-text makes the pattern invisible.
