# Free Product Nurture — internal

Four emails after someone downloads the Starter Kit. Then stop.

**Not outbound.** These go to people who asked for something and got it. Different register: no
pitch in the first two, and the product isn't mentioned until email four.

---

### Day 0 — `here's the starter kit`
```
Here you go — the PDF and the tracker are attached.

If you want the shortest path: open the tracker, pick one building type in
one suburb, and add twenty accounts. The filters on that tab will cut it down
fast.

Hope it's useful.

— [name]
```
Deliver and get out of the way. No pitch, no "over the coming days," no course correction.

### Day 3 — `the one I'd start with`
```
Quick note — of the 25 triggers in there, the one I'd start with is new
occupancy.

Your county permit portal will have it. Search finaled occupancy permits in
the last 60 days, commercial only. Those are buildings that don't have a
cleaner yet, rather than buildings you'd have to take one from.

It's the only trigger on the list where there's no incumbent.
```
One genuinely useful thing. No ask.

### Day 7 — `did you get a list built?`
```
Did you get a chance to build a list?

Asking because I'd like to know whether the filters are cutting too hard. A
couple of people have said twenty accounts turns into about five, which feels
about right to me — but you'd know better than I would.
```

**This is the most important email of the four.** It asks rather than sells, and the replies tell
you whether the kit is being *used* — which is the only thing that predicts whether the paid
product is worth buying.

⚠ **The second sentence is a placeholder.** Do not send *"a couple of people have said"* until a
couple of people have actually said it. Until then: *"Asking because I'd like to know whether the
filters are cutting too hard — my guess is twenty turns into about five, but you'd know better."*

### Day 12 — `the rest of it`
```
Last one from me.

The kit covers finding accounts and sending a first email. The full system is
what happens after that — scoring, reply handling, what to measure on a
walkthrough, how to price it two ways, and the proposal structure.

It's here if it's useful: [link]

Either way, good luck with the list.
```

---

## Rules

**Four, then stop.** They bought or they didn't.

**No fake proof.** No download counts, no testimonials, no results. There aren't any.

**No urgency.** It was free. There's nothing to be urgent about.

**Anyone who replies leaves the sequence** and gets a human answer the same day. A reply is worth
more than the remaining emails.

**Opt-outs suppress globally**, same list as outbound.

## What to watch

| Signal | Reading |
|---|---|
| Day-7 replies | The kit is being used. The number that matters. |
| Day-12 clicks with no purchase | The gap between free and paid is priced wrong, or the page is |
| Downloads with no replies at all | Filed and forgotten. The kit isn't landing, whatever the download count says |

**Twenty downloads and five replies beats a thousand downloads and silence.**
