# The Free Product

**Status: built 2026-09-11.** Files in `../../free-product/`. This document is the spec; the
built kit is the thing.

Top of the funnel, and the thing that makes outbound work without pitching.

**The rule it has to pass:** a cleaning company owner who downloads it and never buys anything
should still be glad they did. If it reads as a teaser, it fails.

---

## What it is

### **The Commercial Cleaning Prospecting Starter Kit**

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | One PDF and one spreadsheet |
| Price | Free, email required |
| Delivery | Gumroad free product, or a simple page + email |
| Job | Prove the method works before asking for money |

### Contents

**1. 25 Commercial Cleaning Buying Triggers** *(PDF, ~8 pages)*
The eleven from the paid Trigger Library plus fourteen more, each with where to find it and how
fresh it stays. **No claim table, no scoring, no freshness discipline** — those are in the paid
product, and they are what turn a list of triggers into a method.

**2. A one-tab account tracker** *(Excel)*
Twenty rows, the qualification filters, and a simple score. The full eleven-tab tracker is the
paid product. This one is genuinely usable for a first list of twenty accounts.

**3. Three outreach templates** *(in the PDF)*
New location, move, and facilities hire — the three highest-value triggers. Written to the same
standard as the paid ones.

### Why this split is honest

The free product gives a complete, working slice: find twenty accounts, spot a trigger, send an
email. Someone can run that and get a conversation.

**What it doesn't give** is the system around it — scoring bands, the claim table, the eleven-tab
tracker, reply handling, the walkthrough checklist, the pricing method, the proposal structure,
the follow-up cadence, the scoreboard.

The free product answers *"does this work?"* The paid product answers *"how do I run this every
week without it falling apart?"* That is a real difference, not a paywall placed mid-sentence.

## How it fits the funnel

```
Cold email  →  "want the list?"  →  reply
      →  send a real trigger-matched pack for their city
      →  plus the Starter Kit
      →  email nurture
      →  the OS at $149
```

**The free product is not the first touch.** The first touch is still the offer of a
trigger-matched list for their own city — that is personal and specific, and a generic download is
neither. The Starter Kit goes out *with* the pack, so they get something immediate and something
reusable.

**It also works standalone** for organic distribution — in a post, a community, a signature — where
there is no pack to build.

## The nurture after download

Four emails. Then stop.

| Day | Subject | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | `here's the starter kit` | The files. One line on where to start. Nothing else. |
| 3 | `the one trigger I'd start with` | New occupancy — why it's the strongest, how to find it in their county |
| 7 | `did you get a list built?` | A real question. Replies here are the most valuable thing the funnel produces |
| 12 | `the rest of it` | What's in the paid product and what it costs. One link. |

**Day 7 matters most.** It asks rather than sells, and the answers tell you whether the free
product is actually being used — which is the only signal that predicts whether the paid one is
worth buying.

---

## What it must not do

**No artificial cliff.** Nothing in it should end with "and the rest is in the paid version." Each
piece is complete on its own terms.

**No fake proof.** No download counts, no testimonials, no results. There aren't any yet.

**No urgency.** It is a free download. There is nothing to be urgent about.

**No fourth-plus email.** Four, then they either bought or they didn't.

## How to tell if it works

| Signal | Reading |
|---|---|
| Downloads | Traffic is arriving |
| Day-7 replies | It is being *used*, which is the number that matters |
| Free → paid conversion | The gap between free and paid is priced right |
| Nothing at all | The offer or the audience is wrong, not the copy |

**Do not judge it on downloads.** A thousand downloads and no replies means a thousand people filed
it and forgot. Twenty downloads and five replies is a better result and a clearer signal.
