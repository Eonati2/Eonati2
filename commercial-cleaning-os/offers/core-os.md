## What it is, in one sentence

A method and a working tracker for finding commercial accounts worth pursuing, identifying who
signs, spotting a dated public reason to make contact, and running that through to a walkthrough.

## What it is not

Not a lead list — no contacts are included. Not a course — no videos, no login. Not a guarantee of
clients. Not a marketing ebook.

---

## Why $149 at launch

The market has commercial-cleaning digital products at $15, $49.99, $149, $249 and $1,941. The
ones at the upper end are sold by people with ratings and a track record. **We have neither yet.**

Getting the first ten real buyers is worth more than defending a launch price. The price rises
when there is proof to justify it, not before.

⚠ **Check the live listing first.** The product is already on Gumroad at $197. If it has no sales,
moving to $149 costs nothing. **If it has sales, hold $197** — dropping the price on early buyers
is a bad trade for a few dollars, and the free product is the better volume lever.

## Why no tiers

The $497 Setup and $2,000 Managed Pilot were removed on 2026-09-11
(`../../research/12-decision-record-v4.md`).

Three reasons, in order of weight: they created a delivery obligation we had never tested; the tier
carrying the revenue target had no purchase path and stayed blocked for three sessions; and one
product makes the whole business legible — one thing to sell, one price, one funnel.

**Do not reintroduce a tier because a prospect asks for help.** If that happens more than a handful
of times, that is evidence worth acting on deliberately, with a decision record. One person asking
is not.

## Positioning

> **Find the commercial accounts worth calling — and the reason to call them now.**

The category is **prospecting infrastructure for commercial cleaning companies.** Not
cleaning-business education, not general marketing, not operations. The market already has all
three.

**The wedge:** most commercial buildings already have a cleaner and won't switch for a better
email. They switch when something changes — a move, a second location, a new management company, a
facilities hire. Those moments are public. Almost nobody in this industry looks for them.

## Who it's for

Commercial cleaning and janitorial companies that can already service recurring B2B accounts and
want more of them. A few crews, some commercial work already won, and new contracts arriving
unpredictably.

## Who it's not for

Residential-only cleaners · anyone wanting guaranteed income · anyone wanting a lead list · anyone
unwilling to qualify accounts and follow up.

## Objections, and the honest answers

**"Why isn't there a cheaper version?"** Because a cheaper version would be the guides without the
tracker, and the tracker is the part that makes it work.

**"Can you set it up for me?"** No. This is a product, not a service. *(If this comes up
repeatedly, log it. Do not improvise a service on a call.)*

**"Does it guarantee clients?"** No, and nothing can. It is a method for finding and qualifying
accounts and getting to walkthroughs.

**"Who else uses it?"** Nobody yet — you'd be among the first. That is true, it is why the launch
price is $149, and it survives being checked. **Never invent a customer.**
