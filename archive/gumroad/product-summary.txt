Commercial Cleaning Client Acquisition OS

An operating system for commercial cleaning and janitorial companies that want a
repeatable way to find commercial accounts, reach the person who decides, and turn
conversations into walkthroughs.

A list tells you who exists. It doesn't tell you which buildings are worth your
time, who signs the cleaning contract, or whether there's a reason for them to take
your call this month.

A list is not a pipeline.

Most commercial buildings already have a cleaner. They switch when something
changes -- a move, a second location, a new building under management, a facilities
hire. Those moments are visible in public records. This is the system for finding
them and acting on them in order:

ACCOUNT -> PERSON -> TRIGGER -> OUTREACH -> FOLLOW-UP -> WALKTHROUGH

Fourteen files. Two working Excel tools -- an eleven-tab Account & Trigger
Tracker with scoring, stages and follow-up flags, and a Weekly Metrics
Scoreboard. Plus twelve guides: trigger library, ICP and targeting, the decision-maker
guide, the prospecting workflow, six outreach sequences, reply handling, the
walkthrough system with a two-method pricing model, the proposal system, a
30-day plan, and an automation blueprint.

For: commercial cleaning companies that can already service recurring B2B accounts.
Not for: residential-only operators, or anyone wanting a lead list or guaranteed
income.

No contacts included. No subscription. No guarantee of clients -- no system can
offer one.
