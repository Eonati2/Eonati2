# Personas — Which Person

Account first, person second. A title is not a decision-maker
(`.claude/skills/revenue-engine/SKILL.md` §5).

---

## Primary — Property Manager / Senior Property Manager

**Owns:** the operational calendar. Tenant relationships, renewals, the day-to-day of the
building.

**Feels the pain directly.** If a notice window closes unnoticed, it lands on this person's desk
first and it is visible to their boss.

**Their day:** reactive. Tenant calls, vendor issues, inspections. Lease-date review is the task
that gets postponed, which is exactly why the deadline gets missed.

**Buys with:** a company card, at $199, usually without a formal approval. This is the reason the
price works.

**Opening that lands:** how they currently keep an eye on what is rolling. Not a pitch.

## Secondary — Asset Manager / Portfolio Manager

**Owns:** the numbers. Rollover concentration, NOI exposure, what the year looks like.

**Cares about Tab 4 and Tab 5**, where the primary persona cares about Tab 3. Same product, a
different first screen.

**Buys with:** more scrutiny and a clearer sense of what software costs, which makes $199 read as
cheap rather than as an expense.

## Tertiary — Director of Property Management / Director of Asset Management

**Owns:** the process across a team. Buys for others, which is a different conversation and a
slower one.

**Worth reaching only when** the firm is at the larger end of the ICP band. Do not lead here — a
director forwards it to a property manager, and we could have started there.

## Not our buyer

| Title | Why |
|---|---|
| Leasing Broker / Leasing Agent | Transacts deals; does not administer the calendar |
| Lease Administrator at a large firm | Already has the software. This is who the software was bought for. |
| CFO / Controller | Cares about the lease accounting standard, which this product deliberately does not touch |
| Facilities / Building Engineer | Different job entirely |

**`Lease Administrator` is the trap.** The title sounds like a perfect match and usually means the
opposite: a dedicated administrator exists because the portfolio outgrew spreadsheets. Treat it as
a disqualifying signal at the account level until proven otherwise.

## What every persona has in common

They all already have the data. The rent roll exists. The lease dates are somewhere.

**We are not selling information. We are selling the arrangement of information they already
own** — which is why the free version can be genuinely useful without giving away the product,
and why "paste your rent roll" is a five-minute onboarding rather than a data-entry project.
