## In one sentence

Paste your rent roll and see which leases need attention before a notice window closes.

## What it is not

Not lease administration software. Not a lease abstraction service. Not legal or compliance
advice. Not a lease accounting tool — it deliberately does not touch ASC 842 or IFRS 16.

---

## Why $199, and why there is no launch discount

The name and the buyer changed on 2026-09-12. The pricing arithmetic did not, because it is
product-independent:

| Price | Sales for $10,000 gross | Qualified downloads the list must produce |
|---|---|---|
| $149 | 68 | ~1,770–6,000 |
| **$199** | **51** | **~1,200–4,300** |

A lower price means more sales, which means more audience to build. No channel currently produces
a single download, so halving what has to be produced is worth more than a discount is. Price was
the largest free lever available on the previous product and it is the same lever here.

**The honest counter-signal.** In the competitive scan, every comparable with a visible sales
count sits low: $49 with 813 sales, $59 with 129 sales. There is no evidence of volume above $149
in that sample.

Two reasons it does not overturn $199 — and the condition that would:

1. Those are **residential landlord** products sold to individuals. Ours is B2B, expensed on a
   company card by someone whose employer pays them far more than $199 a day.
2. A problem whose software tier pays **$532.81 per click** is not a $49 problem.
3. **If $199 produces no sales against a real test — meaning the offer reached enough qualified
   people to conclude anything — price is the first variable to move.** On data, not in advance,
   and not before the audience exists to test it against.

## Why no tiers

Same discipline that removed the service tiers from the previous product on 2026-09-11, and it
fits better here. There is one artifact. A "pro" version would either cripple the base or pad the
top, and both are visible to the buyer.

The free Lite version is the only other SKU and it is a front-end, not a tier.

---

## The claim boundary

**Never claim:** that this prevents a missed deadline · that it protects the buyer legally · that
it guarantees a renewal, an NOI outcome, or any dollar result · that it replaces counsel or a
lease administrator.

**Say instead:** it puts every notice date in one place, sorted by what is closest, so nothing
depends on remembering. Then the disclaimer: confirm every term against the executed lease and
qualified counsel.

The difference is not cosmetic. The first version sells a legal outcome we cannot deliver and
would be liable for. The second sells an arrangement of information, which is exactly what it is.
