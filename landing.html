09  Questions

Before you buy.

Straight answers about what this is, what it is not, and what you can do with it.

Is this a physical product?

No. It is a digital download. Nothing ships. Your files are available immediately after purchase.

Can I edit it?

Yes. The editable source is provided as a PowerPoint file, intended for import into Canva. You can also edit it directly in PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides if you prefer.

Can I customize names, dates and details?

Yes — that is the whole design. Names, dates, times, venues, addresses, hotel details, dress code, contacts, links and recommendations are all placeholder text for you to replace.

Can I print it?

Yes. The itinerary, guest essentials page and QR card are built for print, alongside the mobile guide. Export to PDF and print at home or with a print shop.

Is this a Canva template?

Not in the sense of a one-click Canva template link. It is a PowerPoint source file designed to be imported into Canva, where it becomes fully editable. The START HERE guide covers the import, which takes a couple of minutes.

Can I use it for my own wedding?

Yes, subject to the included personal-use license. You can use and edit it for your own wedding. You cannot resell, redistribute or share the files themselves.

Do I need design experience?

No. The layout, typography and spacing are already set. You are replacing text, not designing pages.

What exactly is in the download?

The editable PowerPoint source containing the mobile guest guide, wedding weekend itinerary, wedding-day timeline, venue and transportation pages, accommodation page, dress code, what to bring, local recommendations, FAQ, important contacts, quick links, printable guest essentials page, QR card and welcome page — plus a START HERE PDF and your personal-use license.
