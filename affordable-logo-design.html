What is the difference between cheap and affordable logo design?
Cheap means low price achieved by reducing quality — templates, no vectors, no rights, no durability. Affordable means low price achieved by reducing overhead, not quality. Fiverr Pro designers charge less than agencies because they have no studio rent, account managers, or pitch costs — not because they produce inferior work. What should I never accept from a budget logo designer?
Never accept a logo delivered only as JPEG or PNG without vector source files. Never accept unclear commercial rights. Never accept a design built from stock elements without disclosure.
