Questions

What is the difference between cheap and affordable logo design?
Cheap means low price achieved by reducing quality — templates, no vectors, no rights, no durability. Affordable means low price achieved by reducing overhead, not quality. Fiverr Pro designers charge less than agencies because they have no studio rent, account managers, or pitch costs — not because they produce inferior work.
